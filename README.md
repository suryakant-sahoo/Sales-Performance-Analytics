# Sales Performance & Customer Analytics

### End-to-End SQL + Power BI Business Analytics Case Study

A business analytics project designed to evaluate sales performance, profitability, customer contribution, regional trends, product performance, and discount impact using SQL and Power BI.

**Project Scale**
- 9,994 transaction-level records
- $2.30M Total Sales
- $286.40K Total Profit
- 12.5% Overall Profit Margin
- SQL-based analytical framework
- Interactive Power BI dashboard
- Executive-level business reporting

 ## Business Problem

Sales leadership needs a consolidated view of revenue performance, profitability, customer contribution, regional performance, product performance, and discount impact.

The objective of this project was to transform transaction-level sales data into actionable business insights that can help stakeholders:

- Identify major revenue and profit drivers
- Understand sales and profitability trends
- Evaluate regional and country performance
- Identify high-value customers and customer concentration
- Compare product sales against profitability
- Detect high-sales but low-profit areas
- Evaluate the relationship between discounts and profitability
- Identify opportunities for revenue growth and margin improvement



## Business Questions Answered

This analysis addresses the following key business questions:

1. What is the overall sales, profit, quantity, and profit margin?
2. How are sales and profit changing over time?
3. Which regions and countries generate the highest revenue?
4. Which regions deliver the strongest profitability?
5. Which products generate the highest sales and profit?
6. Which customers contribute the most revenue and profit?
7. Are there customers with high sales but low or negative profit?
8. Which customer segments and industries drive revenue?
9. How does discounting affect profitability?
10. What percentage of customers contributes to the majority of revenue?
11. Which products perform best within each region?
12. What is the month-over-month sales growth?


## Solution Approach

The project follows an end-to-end analytics workflow:

↓

### 1. Data Profiling
Reviewed record counts, data types, regions, countries, products, and potential data-quality issues.

### 2. Data Validation
Validated key fields, quantities, discounts, dates, and categorical dimensions.

### 3. SQL Analysis
Performed aggregations, KPI calculations, segmentation, profitability analysis, and business-rule analysis.

### 4. Advanced SQL Analytics
Applied CTEs, window functions, RANK(), LAG(), running totals, cumulative contribution, Pareto analysis, and month-over-month growth.

### 5. Power BI
Built an interactive dashboard for executive KPI monitoring, comparative analysis, profitability analysis, and regional/customer performance.

### 6. Business Reporting
Converted analytical findings into business insights and recommendations for stakeholders.


## Key Business Insights

- Generated approximately $2.30M in total sales across 9,994 transactions.
- Generated approximately $286.40K in total profit, resulting in an overall profit margin of approximately 12.5%.
- Identified regional and country-level differences in revenue contribution and profitability.
- Identified high-value customers contributing disproportionately to overall revenue.
- Identified areas with high sales but comparatively weaker profitability.
- Analyzed the impact of discount levels on profit performance.
- Applied Pareto analysis to evaluate customer revenue concentration.
- Used month-over-month growth analysis to identify changes in sales momentum.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions can be considered:

- Prioritize high-value customers and strengthen retention strategies.
- Investigate high-sales but low-profit customers/products for margin leakage.
- Review discounting strategies where higher discounts do not generate proportional profitability.
- Focus sales efforts on high-performing regions and countries while investigating underperforming markets.
- Use monthly growth trends to support sales planning and forecasting.
